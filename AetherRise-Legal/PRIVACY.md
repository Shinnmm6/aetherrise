# Privacy Policy for AetherRise
Last Updated: June 12, 2026

AetherRise values your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. Data We Collect
We collect your Discord User ID. This is used solely to link your game progress (levels, inventory, and stats) to your account within the game.

2. How We Use Data
Your data is used only to maintain your game state. We do not use your data for marketing, advertising, or analytics.

3. Data Sharing
We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

4. Your Rights
You have the right to request the deletion of your game data. To do so, please contact the developer via Discord or email at shinkwablee@gmail.com.

5. Contact
If you have questions, email us at shinkwablee@gmail.com.
